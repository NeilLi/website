SIM-3 LIVE · PLANAR SIMULATION

3D Kinematic Tree Studio

An interactive, zero-dependency 3D browser workbench powered by our analytical C++ dynamics engine compiled to WebAssembly (ABI 2). Inspect planar 1-, 2-, and 3-link articulated chains in a 3D orbit view with live matrix readouts.

  • 1-, 2-, and 3-Link Planar Dynamics: Nonlinear pendulum, double pendulum, and 3-DOF articulated robot arm
  • Interactive 3D Orbit Viewport: Zero-dependency perspective projection with drag-to-orbit yaw/pitch and scroll zoom
  • Real-Time Analytical Inspectors: Live Mass Matrix M(q), Coriolis vector, gravity vector g(q) = ∇U, and End-Effector Jacobian J(q)
  • Mechanical Energy Tracking: Inspect kinetic (K), potential (U), and total (E) energy as motors and damping change the system
  • Versioned Model Contracts: Model schema seedcore.mini-sim.tree-model.v1 with canonical SHA-256 digests and JSON export
