WHAT THIS MODEL COVERS

A teaching arm, one research step.

The model is a fixed-base planar arm. It has no collision solver, floating body, robot importer, or Microduck dynamics.

SIM-1 and a narrow SIM-2 C++/Wasm checkpoint slice are implemented and locally measured. Saved progress resumes on the same engine build. Broader browser and device qualification remains open.

Simulation observations are local research data. They do not authorize robot motion or prove hardware behavior.

01 / PROPOSE

The thinking brain

A host or policy proposes a short action sequence for a defined task.

→
02 / ADMIT

The trust boundary

An accountable agent requests permission. Policy decides the scope and duration.

→
03 / ENFORCE

The local controller

The robot checks admitted commands and enforces limits, expiry, and interruption.

→
04 / REVIEW

The observed attempt

Correlated physical telemetry supports independent verification and replay.

This is the intended hardware architecture. Control rates, transport, and stop behavior need qualification for each robot. A small body does not remove physical hazards, and simulation results do not grant execution authority.

NEXT MICRODUCK MILESTONE
