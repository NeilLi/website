Scenes from the studio

One small city. Five human stories.

The homepage illustration is a starting point for the applications I’m developing. These five example scenes show the people, choices, and small outcomes behind each idea.

Picture the moment

A map invites a detour. A kiosk invites a design. A family follows a clue. A visitor discovers a handmade piece. A robot joins the table. Choose a scene and imagine it in your own setting.

The stories behind the scenes

What might a better moment feel like?

01 · Digital City

“What’s around the next corner?”

A family has an hour before lunch. They ask for a nearby place to explore and find a small workshop along a walkable route. The maker’s story gives them a reason to stop.

The idea: help people turn local curiosity into a visit, with enough context to choose a place for themselves.

02 · Tourist Design Studio

“Can I put our adventure on a T-shirt?”

After a visit, a child chooses a local landmark and adds a creature from their imagination. At the creative kiosk, the family tries a few colors and previews the design together.

The idea: make the keepsake part of the experience, with an adult reviewing the design and price before an order.

03 · Family Journey

“We found the next clue!”

A story gives a family a reason to notice the little things: a pattern on a building, an animal in an exhibit, or a detail along the path. Each small discovery moves the adventure forward.

The idea: help children and grown-ups explore together, with choices and challenges connected to the place they are visiting.

Explore Family Journey → Discuss a similar scene ↗
04 · Craft & Collectibles

“Who made this, and what makes it special?”

A visitor notices a hand-glazed vase beside a woven textile and a carved wooden bird. They learn about the workshop, the materials, and the hands behind each piece before choosing something to take home.

The idea: help a maker’s story travel with the object, together with care instructions and useful notes about its condition and journey.

Explore Craft & Collectibles → Discuss a similar scene ↗
05 · Robot Moments

“Would you like a little help?”

At a shared table, a small robot introduces a creative activity and waits for a response. It guides one step, then leaves the next choice to the person sitting beside it.

The idea: explore friendly, useful interactions that fit into a room and keep people involved.
