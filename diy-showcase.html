Primary Reference Embodiment

Microduck Biped

An open-source bipedal robot selected as SeedCore’s primary reference embodiment. Compact 0.737 kg body, 14 degrees of freedom, and 38 3D-printable parts.

Ingestion Status: Pinned Microduck 0.1.0 MJCF package verified with SHA-256 integrity manifest. Active milestone M0/M1 establishes the 61/14 tensor interface and zero-drift startup simulation.

3-Link Articulated Manipulator

Interactive 3-DOF revolute serial chain running in the browser on our zero-allocation C++/Wasm analytical dynamics kernel (ABI 2). Full 3D perspective orbit controls and live matrix inspectors.

Qualification Status: Fully qualified (SIM-3) with 78/78 tests passing. Live M(q), Coriolis, J(q), and mechanical energy conservation verified.

Maker Reference

SO-100 / Tabletop Arm

The popular 3D-printed leader-follower robotic arm driven by low-cost Feetech STS3215 magnetic encoder serial bus servos. Ideal for desktop teleoperation and ACT training.

Hardware Bridge: Runs on our ESP32 Fast Spine firmware with WebSerial browser deployment. Passive leader arms allow rapid dataset collection without expensive motion capture.
