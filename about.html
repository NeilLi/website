SeedCore’s proposed service starts with a person’s desired outcome: understand the need, compare hardware, define a bounded function, validate the result, and plan support. Customer discovery runs alongside engineering. A useful robot is a means to an outcome.

THE FIRST ENGINEERING PROOF

One move we can explain.

Microduck is the reference integration. The immediate goal is a short bounded move followed by a stop, with a trace of the accountable requester, permission, physical attempt, and observed result. The native workbench now inspects a pinned Microduck model package. Its hardware adapter and governed motion remain planned; the browser arm is a separate teaching model.
