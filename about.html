About SeedCore

Applications for places, journeys and real things.

I’m building SeedCore around the moments in the homepage illustration: choosing a place to explore, making a souvenir, sharing an adventure, discovering a handmade piece, and sitting down with a friendly robot.

Where it comes to life

The map, creative kiosk, craft display, and robot table are starting points for application ideas. Each begins with a person, a setting, and something they want to do.

How I approach the work

Start with the person. Make the next step clear.

01 · Begin with a moment

Grounded in the real world.

A family needs a next stop. A child wants to make something. A visitor wants to know who made a bowl and how to care for it. Those everyday questions give the work its direction.

02 · Keep people involved

People hold the keys.

Suggestions should leave room for a choice. An adult reviews a souvenir order, a buyer checks the details of a handmade piece, and a person chooses the robot’s next activity.

03 · Keep it considered

Keep what matters.

My aim is to ask only for information an experience needs, explain how it is used, and let people choose what to keep. A family’s memory should remain theirs to share.
