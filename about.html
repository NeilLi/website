THE PHYSICAL PARADIGM SHIFT

Why mini-robotics changes everything.

Small robot bodies make compact, supervised experiments practical. Lower mass can reduce collision energy, but actuator torque, pinch points, speed, power rails, and the workspace still need evaluation. SeedCore starts with simulation and explicit limits; physical safety requires measured qualification of each build.

  • Small Robot Bodies
  • Bounded Motion
  • Supervised Bench Checks
  • Rapid Iteration
THE BEHAVIORAL CHALLENGE

Why trust requires cryptographic proof.

AI agents and vision-language models propose actions that affect workspaces, hardware, and network services. SeedCore makes permission and evidence explicit through single-use session tokens, revocation fencing, watchdog bounds, and tamper-evident receipts. The delivered rehearsal exercises these controls in host simulation; a token or verified receipt does not establish physical safety.

  • Single-Use Session Tokens
  • Revocation Fencing
  • Authority Epoch Invalidation
  • Python & Rust Receipt Verifiers

THE TRUST LIFECYCLE

From canonical assembly & intent to verifiable evidence.

How SeedCore separates offline proxy authoring, policy admission, bounded session execution, and receipt verification.

01 / AUTHOR & PROPOSE

Mini Studio & Planner

Authors a canonical seedcore.assembly.v2 graph (CW-A01–A03) or proposes a bounded ActionIntent chunk with velocity, angle, and horizon limits.

→
02 / ADMIT

SeedCore Trust Runtime

The session rehearsal checks authority, profile bounds, and freshness before issuing a single-use token. Separately, the offline authoring store increments authorityEpoch on content changes and keeps admission null; live assembly admission is a later integration gate.

→
03 / REHEARSE & GOVERN

Fast Spine Boundary

Executes bounded fast-spine-v1 simulation sessions (or target MCU interpolation once CW-D01/G1 pass) with independent watchdog fencing.

→
04 / VERIFY

Python & Rust Kernels

Validates session tokens, signer provenance, and action-bound receipts, quarantining missing, stale, or conflicting telemetry.

Authoring revisions and simulation rehearsals never grant physical hardware authority (physical_acceptance: false). Captured hardware evidence requires an enrolled device signer (G2).
